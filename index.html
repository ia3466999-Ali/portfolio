About Imran Ali

I am an undergraduate scholar pursuing my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Presidency University, Bengaluru (2026 – 2030).

I completed my 10th from CBSE and 12th from Bihar Board (BSEB) with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM), English, and Hindi. Driven by a passion for building scalable software, I mastered full-stack development through professional MERN Stack training at Programming Classes, Patna.

Alongside engineering, I write classical and contemporary Urdu/Hindi poetry under the takhallus ‘Ali’, and serve as an active volunteer and stage orator with the National Service Scheme (NSS).
