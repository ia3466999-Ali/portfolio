About Imran Ali

I am a first-year undergraduate pursuing my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Presidency University, Bengaluru.

My engineering work bridges analytical rigor and practical utility — whether building EcoPluse to combat event food waste, engineering glacier flood warning telemetry, or developing desktop voice interfaces.

Alongside code, I write classical and contemporary Urdu/Hindi poetry under the takhallus ‘Ali’. As an active volunteer and stage presenter with the National Service Scheme (NSS), I've brought verses of student struggle, youth ambition, and love to college and community audiences.
